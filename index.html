Our Mission

Not a lack of ability.
A lack of access.

Young Latinos in Urbana-Champaign aren't falling behind because they aren't capable. They fall behind because the systems around them weren't designed with them in mind. Adelante changes that — pairing students with mentors who look like them, understand their world, and speak their language.

We start in middle school, because waiting until high school is waiting too long.

Read Our Full Story →

The Challenge

The barriers are real.
So is our response.

Latino youth face compounding inequities that begin early. Adelante was built to interrupt that cycle before it takes root.

🏫

Early Disengagement

Disengagement patterns start as early as 2nd grade. Without early intervention, they harden into habits — limiting opportunities before students even discover them.

🤝

No Cultural Bridge

Most programs aren't designed with Latino youth in mind. When students can't connect culturally with their mentor, the relationship fails before it begins.

🗺️

Invisible Pathways

College, trade school, tech careers — many paths are invisible without early exposure. Adelante makes them visible, concrete, and belonging to every student.

Our Programs

Three pillars.
One student at a time.

Not one-size-fits-all. Built to grow with each student from 6th grade through graduation day.

Pillar 01

Long-Term Mentorship

Paired with a consistent mentor from UIUC, alumni, or local trade pros — matched on values, trained in cultural responsiveness. Years, not weeks.

Learn more →

Pillar 02

Career Exploration

Workshops, guest speakers, and site visits expose students to college, trade, technical, and professional pathways — broadening what success can mean.

Learn more →

Pillar 03

Family & Community

We partner with families and local schools — because the strongest support systems wrap around a student's whole life, not just their school hours.
