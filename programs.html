Our Programs

Three pillars.
One student
at a time.

Relationship-driven, culturally informed, and built to grow with each student from 6th grade through graduation.

01

Pillar One

Long-Term,
Culturally Aware Mentorship

Each student is paired with a consistent mentor — a UIUC student, alumni, or local professional — matched on shared values, interests, and background. Mentors complete cultural responsiveness training so they can show up fully for their students.

The relationship spans years, not weeks. Monthly one-on-one sessions give students a consistent presence from middle school through senior year.

02

Pillar Two

Career Exploration
& Workshops

Students attend structured workshops, career panels, and on-site visits throughout the year. Guest speakers from local businesses, UIUC, and trade programs walk students through the many roads to a fulfilling career.

By exposing students to college, trade school, technical certifications, and skilled trades early, we help them make informed decisions about their futures.

03

Pillar Three

Family &
Community Integration

We partner with families and local schools — because the strongest support systems wrap around a student's whole life, not just their school hours. Parents and guardians are invited to workshops, events, and regular check-ins.

Community events bring together students, families, mentors, and local professionals — building the network of support many of our students have never had before.

The Journey

How it works,
step by step.

From first application to graduation day — here's what the Adelante journey looks like.

1

Apply

Students or families submit an application. We learn about interests, goals, and what support would help most.

2

Get Matched

We pair each student with a mentor based on shared values, interests, and background — not just availability.

3

Meet & Grow

Monthly one-on-ones begin. Students attend workshops, career events, and campus visits throughout the year.

4

Graduate Ready

By senior year, students have a plan, a network, and the confidence to pursue any path they choose.

Who Can Join

Are you eligible?

🌱Students

  • Currently enrolled in grades 6 through 12
  • Living in the Champaign-Urbana area
  • Latino, Hispanic, or from an underrepresented background
  • Willing to commit to regular monthly sessions
  • Parent or guardian support and involvement
  • No prior experience or special qualifications needed

⭐Mentors

  • UIUC student (junior or senior), alumni, or local professional
  • Able to commit to one meeting per month minimum
  • Complete Adelante's cultural responsiveness training
  • Background check required
  • Bilingual (Spanish/English) preferred but not required
  • Passionate about supporting underrepresented youth
