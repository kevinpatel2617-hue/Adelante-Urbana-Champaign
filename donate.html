Why your gift matters here.

Adelante serves students right here in Champaign-Urbana — kids who are capable of anything but need someone to show them the path. Your donation makes that possible.

Mentored youth are 55% more likely to enroll in college and 78% more likely to give back to their communities. You're not just funding a program — you're changing a family's trajectory.

Adelante UC is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit. All donations are fully tax-deductible. You will receive a confirmation and tax receipt via email after submitting your pledge.
