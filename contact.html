FAQ

Common questions,
answered.

What grades are eligible for Adelante?
▾
Adelante is open to students in grades 6 through 12 in the Champaign-Urbana area. We specifically prioritize 6th grade for new enrollment, as early intervention is central to our model.
Does my child need to be Latino to join?
▾
Adelante primarily serves Latino youth, but is open to any underrepresented student in the C-U area who would benefit from culturally informed mentorship.
Is there a cost to join the program?
▾
Adelante is completely free for all students and families. Our programs are funded by donations, grants, and community partnerships so cost is never a barrier.
How much time does mentorship require?
▾
Students and mentors meet at least once per month. Students are also encouraged to attend quarterly workshops and events — roughly 2–4 hours per month total.
Do I need experience to become a mentor?
▾
No prior experience required. Adelante provides comprehensive cultural responsiveness training for all mentors. The only requirement is a genuine commitment to showing up for a young person.
Do you need to speak Spanish to mentor?
▾
Spanish is preferred but not required. Many of our students are bilingual, and monolingual English mentors are absolutely welcome.
How are donations used?
▾
Donations fund program coordination, mentor training, student workshop materials, family events, and operational costs — all directed toward direct student impact.
Can businesses or organizations partner with Adelante?
▾
Absolutely. We partner with local businesses, schools, faith organizations, and civic groups. Send us a message above and our team will reach out to discuss partnership opportunities.
