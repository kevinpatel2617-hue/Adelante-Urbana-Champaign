About Adelante

Built for a community
that deserved
better.

Adelante Urbana-Champaign was created because the Latino students who needed guidance the most were receiving it the least — and that is not acceptable.

🌟

Our Mission

Adelante Urbana-Champaign equips primarily Latino youth with early, culturally informed mentorship and structured academic and career support — broadening access to long-term opportunities in pursuit of generational success.

🌿

Our Vision

A community where Latino and underrepresented youth have equal access to guidance, opportunities, and support systems — allowing them to reach their full potential, eliminate generational barriers, and pursue any path they choose.

Why Adelante Exists

The barriers are real.
And they start early.

Latino youth in Urbana-Champaign face compounding inequities — not from lack of ability, but from a lack of access to the right support at the right time.

📚

Systemic Barriers

Many Latino students navigate cultural barriers, limited family familiarity with the U.S. education system, and fewer visible examples of professionals in high-paying fields — making every step harder without support.

🔗

Missing Cultural Connection

Mentorship only works when it's culturally aware. Without mentors who understand a student's lived experience, trust never forms — and without trust, guidance never lands.

⏱️

Too Late, Too Little

Most support programs engage students in 11th or 12th grade — after disengagement patterns are entrenched. Adelante intervenes in 6th grade, when it actually changes outcomes.

Our Origin

Born from
lived experience.

Adelante was created by someone who grew up watching Latino classmates disengage — not because they weren't capable, but because no one showed them the path. Growing up in a bilingual education environment made the systemic barriers impossible to ignore.

The program is built on one simple truth — culturally informed, early mentorship works. Research proves it. Students who've had it can show you. Adelante was built to make sure every young person in Urbana-Champaign has that chance.

Our Values

What we stand for, every day.

🌱

Early Intervention

The earlier a student is supported, the more profound and lasting the impact. Waiting until high school is waiting too long.

🤝

Cultural Responsiveness

Every mentor is trained to understand the lived experience of Latino youth — their challenges, their families, and the specific barriers they navigate.

🗺️

Expanded Pathways

Success is not one road. We expose students to college, trade, technical, and entrepreneurial paths — letting them choose a future that belongs to them.

🏡

Family & Community

Families are partners, not bystanders. We involve parents in every step because the strongest support systems are built together.
