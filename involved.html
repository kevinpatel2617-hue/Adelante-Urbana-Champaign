Adelante is for Latino and underrepresented students in grades 6–12 in Urbana-Champaign. No experience needed — just the willingness to show up and move forward.

What you get:

  • A consistent long-term mentor matched to your interests and background
  • Monthly sessions focused on your goals — academic and personal
  • Career workshops, campus visits, and professional exposure
  • A community of students and families who have your back
